🔬 Guided Investigation

Amplitude: Taller waves generally carry more energy when other factors stay the same.

Frequency: Higher frequency means more waves pass a point in a given time. In this model, increasing frequency makes crests closer together.

Energy transfer: The wave moves across the water, while the buoy mainly bobs up and down. Real surface-water particles move in approximately circular orbits; this model simplifies that motion.
